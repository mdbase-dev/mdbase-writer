# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People who write sustained, cited work — papers, theses, books — and keep
their notes and reading in an mdbase collection, usually alongside mdbase
Reader. They know Markdown and citations; they should not need to know
Typst, CSL or Connect's internals.

## Product purpose

mdbase writer turns Markdown records into typeset documents with correct
citations, while the text stays ordinary Markdown in the user's collection.
Success means a writer drafts in plain text, sees the typeset result as they
type, cites from what they have read, and hands in a PDF or Word file without
leaving their collection.

## Positioning

Overleaf-style immediacy without LaTeX, and without a proprietary project
format: manuscripts are records other apps can read, the bibliography is the
user's Reader library, and the Markdown follows Pandoc/Quarto conventions so it
builds outside the app too.

## Principles

1. The Markdown record is the document; the app is one way to build it.
2. The preview keeps up with typing.
3. Problems appear on the line that caused them, in plain language.
4. Citations match Pandoc's citeproc, so PDF and Word agree.
5. Records are never deleted or renamed by the writer; access stays narrow.

## Brand

The product is `mdbase writer`, in the mdbase visual language defined by
mdbase-connect's DESIGN.md.
