# Coral reefs {#sec-reefs}

Darwin explained atolls by subsidence: a reef that fringes a volcanic island
keeps growing upward as the island slowly sinks [@darwinStructure42].
The idea came from reading Lyell on the Beagle.[^lyell]

[^lyell]: On causes now in operation see @lyellPrinciples30.
