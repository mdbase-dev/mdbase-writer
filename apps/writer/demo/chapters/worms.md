# Earthworms {#sec-worms}

Worms swallow earth below ground and leave it as castings on the surface, so
that objects on the surface are slowly buried [@darwinFormation81, chap. 3].
If castings accumulate at a rate $r$ each year, an object lies at a depth

$$
d = r \, t
$$ {#eq-burial}

after $t$ years. Compare the reefs in @sec-reefs, and see the closing
pages of the book [@darwinFormation81, 313].
