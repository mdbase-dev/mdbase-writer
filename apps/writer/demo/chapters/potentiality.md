# Potentiality {#sec-potentiality}

Agamben takes his bearings from Aristotle's account of *dynamis*: genuine
potentiality includes the potentiality not to [@agambenPotentialities99, 177--84].
The architect who does not build retains the capacity to build.[^capacity]

[^capacity]: On the privative cases see @agambenBartleby99, 253--55.

> What is potential can both be and not be.
> [@agambenPotentialities99, 182]
