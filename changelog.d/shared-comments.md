## Changed

- Comments now use the comment model, panel and editor anchors shared with the other mdbase apps (`@mdbase-dev/ui` 0.1.0-beta.176), in place of Writer's own. Threads, replies, suggested edits, the author and "new since your last visit" filters, margin marks and the composer beside the text all remain. What others have written since the last visit is marked in words on the thread rather than by a fill, and the hover card on a commented passage no longer has a kind heading.
