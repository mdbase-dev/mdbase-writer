## Fixed

- The comments panel no longer shows "Comments could not be loaded" right after adding, replying to, resolving, reopening or withdrawing a comment. Its reload had refused the record the write had just made because that write was still being confirmed.
- The "Your comments are not signed" notice no longer flips between states while your account is linked. An older account check can no longer replace a newer one, and a brief connection change is checked again before Writer says it could not check the account.
